## What it does

Investigate a question against primary sources and leave cited findings in the project’s existing notes convention.

## When to reach for it

Use for requested research, documentation/API facts, or bounded delegated reading. When delegation is unavailable or forbidden, investigate directly.

## Common questions

**Can research agents recursively delegate?**

No. A delegated researcher is a leaf worker and investigates directly without invoking the orchestration again.

## It's working if

- Claims point to the source that owns them.
- The saved findings are discoverable through the project’s conventions.
- Delegation stays within available capacity and authorization.
